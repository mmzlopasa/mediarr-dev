Your photos and videos, on your own server.

Mediarr backs up every phone by itself, keeps the whole family's pictures in one place and finds anything in them. It opens in an instant, and nothing ever leaves your server.

Install on your server Open the demo

Demo sign-in: demo@mediarr.app · mediarr-demo

The photos are the interface. Months are titles, pictures run edge to edge, and everything else steps back until you need it. Originals are never changed, every edit can be undone, and the library is yours, on your own disk.

Search the way you think

Describe what you are looking for in your own words. Filter by date, person, place and camera, and find words inside pictures. It all runs on your server.

See where you have been

Photos appear on a map, places are worked out offline, and trips are put together for you. Hide the location of anything you share.

A darkroom for each picture

A dark stage, the photos around it, and the details beside: stars, caption, camera, place and the people in it.

Keep and share what matters

Albums, favourites, an archive and a locked vault. Share with a partner, an album's members or a link, without giving away your originals.

Together on the TV

Memories, albums and people in big rows, made for a remote: arrows, OK and back. A slideshow for the whole room.

Your own disk

Files stay where you put them. Originals are never changed, and edits can always be undone.

Automatic backup

Each phone backs up by itself in the background, so new pictures simply appear.

Bring your library

Import from Immich, Google Takeout and iCloud, or show a folder you already have.

Real backups

Encrypted backups to a folder, S3 or Backblaze B2, with a test that really brings them back.

People

Faces are recognised on your server and grouped by person. Correct one in a tap; names stay private to you.

Stars that travel

Ratings, captions and corrected dates go out as a standard XMP sidecar with the picture.

Albums that fill themselves

Smart albums gather pictures by rules you set, and keep up as new ones arrive.

No domain needed

It runs at your server's address. A domain and HTTPS are up to you.

Up and running in minutes.

All you need is Docker. Download two files, say where your photos go, and start it.

curl -fsSL -o docker-compose.yml https://cdn.mediarr.app/install/docker-compose.yml
curl -fsSL -o .env https://cdn.mediarr.app/install/example.env
docker compose up -d
