Your family's photos and videos, kept on your own server.

Mediarr backs up every phone by itself, puts the whole family's pictures in one library and finds anything in them. Originals are never changed, and nothing leaves your server.

The whole library, one sheet.

Every part of Mediarr, printed as it really looks. The one marked in blue is where each picture ends up.

Each picture gets the whole room.

Open a photo and everything else steps back: a dark stage, quiet actions, a strip of the photos around it, and the details from the right with the stars first, then caption, camera, place and the people in it.

After the shoot, less to look through.

Go through a day of photos quickly, let Mediarr find the copies and the misses, and put wrong dates and places right for many photos at once. Nothing is deleted straight away: what you remove waits in the trash.

Culling a shoot

One photo at a time, with the rest of the shoot in a strip below. Pick it, reject it or give it stars, and compare two to four side by side, zoomed together. When you are done, the rejects go to the trash and the picks become an album.

  • PPick
  • XReject
  • 1–5Stars
  • UClear

On a phone, drag a photo up to pick it and down to reject it.

Duplicates
Copies are grouped, with one proposed to keep and the reason why. The one you keep takes over what the copies had: their albums, tags, favourite, description and the people in them.
Clean up
Blurry photos, screenshots, large videos and the extra shots of a burst, each with the space they take. Favourites and photos in albums are left unticked.
Dates and places
Shift a camera clock that was wrong, give photos the time zone of where they were taken, or place them from nearby photos or a GPX track. A preview shows what will change first.

Made to be kept for decades.

Pictures outlive the phones that took them. Mediarr treats every original as a negative: read only, never touched.

Originals
Never changed. Every edit is kept as a list in the library, and can always be undone.
Every phone
Each phone backs up by itself in the background, so new pictures simply appear.
One library
The whole family's pictures in one place.
People
Faces are recognised on your server and grouped by person. Correct one in a tap.
Analysis
Smart search and faces can run on a stronger computer at home, and the models leave memory when nothing has asked for them for a while.
Your copies
Encrypted backups to a folder, S3 or Backblaze B2, with a test that really brings them back.
Metadata
Ratings, captions and corrected dates go out as a standard XMP sidecar with the picture.
Smart albums
They gather pictures by rules you set, and keep up as new ones arrive.
Coming over
Bring in the exports of the photo services you used before, or show a folder you already have.
Leaving
Export the whole library at any time: every original, its XMP sidecar, your albums and everything Mediarr knows about each picture, in ZIP files any computer opens.

Memories come back. Links share only what you choose.

Memories that find themselves

Besides On this day, Mediarr puts memories together from your own library once a day. Titles are built from your data, in the language you read Mediarr in.

  • Trips away from home
  • A person through the years
  • Birthdays
  • Last year in photos
  • The day a month ago

A link that keeps things private

When you share an album by link, you decide what goes with it, and can change it later.

  • Blur faces: all of them, or all but the people you choose
  • Take out the camera details and the date
  • Ask visitors for their name
  • See who opened it, when, from roughly where, and how much they viewed and downloaded

Two files and one command.

All you need is a server with Docker. Download two files, say where your photos go, and start it.

Needs
Docker
Files
docker-compose.yml and .env
Address
http://your-server:9851
Domain, HTTPS
Not needed. Add them if you like.
mkdir -p ~/mediarr && cd ~/mediarr
curl -fsSL -o docker-compose.yml https://cdn.mediarr.app/install/docker-compose.yml
curl -fsSL -o .env https://cdn.mediarr.app/install/example.env
# set the password and your photo folders in .env, then:
docker compose up -d

On every phone, and on the TV.

Sign in on a new phone by scanning a QR code from a screen where you are already signed in. The app finds the server on your home Wi-Fi by itself. Mediarr speaks English, Croatian, German and Slovenian; German and Slovenian are machine translated for now.

  • iPhone

    Early version, installed with a sideloading app such as Feather.

    Download .ipa

  • Android

    Early version, installed directly from the file.

    Download .apk

  • TV

    Play on TV sends what is on your screen to a Chromecast, an Apple TV or a DLNA TV, and the TV follows as you swipe. Or open TV mode in the TV's browser, made for a remote.

    From the web, Chromecast works in Chrome over HTTPS, and Safari sends videos by AirPlay. Photos reach an Apple TV by Screen Mirroring.

    Try it in the demo
