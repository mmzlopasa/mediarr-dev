Your family's photos and videos, kept on your own server.

Mediarr backs up every phone by itself, puts the whole family's pictures in one library and finds anything in them. Originals are never changed, and nothing leaves your server.

The whole library, one sheet.

Every part of Mediarr, printed as it really looks. The one marked in blue is where each picture ends up.

Each picture gets the whole room.

Open a photo and everything else steps back: a dark stage, quiet actions, a strip of the photos around it, and the details from the right with the stars first, then caption, camera, place and the people in it.

Made to be kept for decades.

Pictures outlive the phones that took them. Mediarr treats every original as a negative: read only, never touched.

Originals
Never changed. Every edit is kept as a list in the library, and can always be undone.
Every phone
Each phone backs up by itself in the background, so new pictures simply appear.
One library
The whole family's pictures in one place.
People
Faces are recognised on your server and grouped by person. Correct one in a tap.
Your copies
Encrypted backups to a folder, S3 or Backblaze B2, with a test that really brings them back.
Metadata
Ratings, captions and corrected dates go out as a standard XMP sidecar with the picture.
Smart albums
They gather pictures by rules you set, and keep up as new ones arrive.
Coming over
Import from Immich, Google Takeout and iCloud, or show a folder you already have.

Two files and one command.

All you need is a server with Docker. Download two files, say where your photos go, and start it.

Needs
Docker
Files
docker-compose.yml and .env
Address
http://your-server:9851
Domain, HTTPS
Not needed. Add them if you like.
curl -fsSL -o docker-compose.yml https://cdn.mediarr.app/install/docker-compose.yml
curl -fsSL -o .env https://cdn.mediarr.app/install/example.env
docker compose up -d
