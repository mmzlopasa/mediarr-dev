# Your profile

Under **Account → Profile** on the web, or **Account → Profile** in the mobile app, you can change:

- **Your picture.** Choose any photo; Mediarr cuts a square from it, around the face where it can. Everyone with an account on the same server sees it, for example beside an album you share. **Remove** goes back to your initials.
- **Your name.**
- **Your email address**, which you sign in with. Changing it asks for your current password.

Files the server could not read, such as a damaged photo, are listed under **Account → Your library**, not over your photos.
