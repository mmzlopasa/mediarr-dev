# Memories

Besides **On this day** and **The last week**, Mediarr finds these kinds of memories by itself, once a day, in the background:

- **Trips**: a run of at least two days away from home with five or more photos, for example "Split, July 2024". Home is the place with the most photos, and a day counts as away only when it is more than 50 km from there. A trip is shown for two weeks after it ends, and again for a week on each anniversary.
- **A person through the years**: for someone you have named, with photos from at least three different years. One person at a time, in turn, each for a week.
- **Birthdays**: on the birthday of a person with a date of birth (set on the person's page), if there are at least three photos of them.
- **A year in photos**: last year, through January.
- **A month ago**: the photos of the day exactly a month back, when there are at least three.

Titles are built from your data and shown in your language: nothing is generated or invented. Memories show your favourites, higher ratings and sharper photos first, and never anything in the trash, the archive or the vault, nor screenshots, rejected photos and duplicates.

## Changing what you see

- In a playing memory, **Hide this memory** hides that memory for good, and **Don't show this person** keeps a person out of every memory.
- Under **Account → Preferences** you can turn each kind of memory off, and see what is hidden and bring it back.

Everything works the same in the web app and in the mobile app.
