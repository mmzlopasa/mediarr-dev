# Duplicates and clean up

Both are under **More** on the web and under **Library** in the app. Nothing is ever deleted at once: what you remove goes to the trash, where it stays for as long as the trash keeps things (30 days unless the administrator changed it) and can be restored.

## Duplicates

Mediarr compares your photos and groups the ones that look alike. Each group says whether it is the **Same picture** (the same file, or the same picture in the same size) or only **Alike**, and how much space removing the copies frees.

One photo of each group is proposed to keep, and the group says why:

1. a favorite;
2. the camera's own file (HEIC, RAW) over a copy an app saved again, such as a WhatsApp copy;
3. more pixels;
4. more that is known about it: a date, a place, a description;
5. a larger file, then the sharper picture.

Tick what to keep and choose **Resolve**. The kept photo takes over what the copies had and it lacks:

- their albums, and their place as an album's cover;
- comments and hearts in shared albums;
- tags, favorite, description, and the highest rating;
- date and location;
- names you gave to faces.

Then the copies go to the trash. **Not duplicates** stops proposing the group.

**Resolve all as proposed** does every group at once, and **Resolve the identical** only the groups of the same picture. Both first show how many groups, how many photos go to the trash and how much space that frees. They run in the background, with a progress bar.

## Clean up

**Clean up** gathers what many libraries no longer need:

| Kind | What it finds |
| --- | --- |
| Blurry photos | Photos with very few sharp edges. A slider sets how strict. |
| Screenshots | By their name in many languages, by the mark an iPhone leaves, or a PNG without a camera in the shape of a phone's screen. |
| Large videos | Videos larger than 250 MB, 500 MB, 1 GB or 2 GB, the largest first. |
| Bursts | The other shots of a burst. The best shot stays. A RAW with its JPEG is not a burst. |

Each kind shows how many items it has and how much space they take. Everything is ticked except favorites and photos in albums, which are shown with a label. Tap to tick or untick. In the app, hold a picture to look at it; on the web, videos have **Watch**.

**Move to the trash** removes the ticked ones. **Keep the selected** keeps them, and Clean up does not offer them again. Until every photo has been analysed, the page says how many are still waiting.
